## Final upload checks

- English audio or English subtitles.
- Pitch video: 2–3 minutes.
- Product demo: no more than 3 minutes.
- 1080p preferred.
- No real recovery words.
- No wallet seed phrase, JWK, admin password, API key, or private family data.
- Every link works in an incognito browser.
- The video is accessible without requesting access.
